## Practical patterns

### Research

Define the decision, object, analyst role, source rules, date range, output schema and evidence checks. Treat a polished answer as a hypothesis until important claims are checked at source.

### Writing

Across multiple samples, identify viewpoint, recurring attention, value order, evidence habits, reader relationship and expression boundaries. Preserve this stable system while allowing structure and wording to change.

### Visuals

Use references as visual language rather than source material to copy. Before adding copy, write what the image alone must prove. Across a set, maintain shared visual DNA but vary subject, distance and proof form.

### Data

Inspect schema, missing values, duplicates, data types and metric definitions before analysis. Analyse business dimensions and changes, then reconcile aggregates against source rows before presentation.
