# Playbook — AI Video Production

## Pre-production

### Brief

```text
Audience:
Channel and ratio:
Duration:
Message:
Desired action:
Brand constraints:
Required product/character:
Budget and deadline:
```

### Character bible

Record face, age, body, hair, clothing, colour, expression range and forbidden changes. Use authorised likeness only.

### Scene bible

Record location, time, palette, light direction, weather, recurring props and continuity rules.

## Shot planning

Each shot contains:

- shot ID and duration;
- narrative function;
- starting composition;
- subject action;
- camera action;
- end composition;
- continuity reference;
- audio/caption need;
- rejection conditions.

## Generation strategy

1. Generate or approve reference frames.
2. Test the hardest shot before the whole sequence.
3. Keep clip length manageable.
4. Reuse identity and scene references.
5. Change one major variable at a time.
6. Record prompt, model, duration, cost and result.

## Clip review

Check at normal speed and frame-by-frame for:

- face and body drift;
- hand, eye and mouth artefacts;
- product deformation;
- disappearing objects;
- light and shadow inconsistency;
- camera/subject motion conflict;
- text mutation;
- unsafe or unlicensed content.

## Edit

Select only shots that advance the story. Trim before the artefact, bridge continuity with cutaways, keep human appearances short when needed and review sound separately.

## Metrics

- generations attempted;
- usable clips;
- usable seconds;
- generation spend;
- correction time;
- rejection reason;
- final effective cost per usable second.

## Final QA

Render the target ratio and inspect the actual exported file for duration, frame size, audio, captions, text safety, colour and playback errors.
