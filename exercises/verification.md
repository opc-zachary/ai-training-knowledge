# Verification Drills

## Drill 1 — Conflicting market numbers

Two reports give different growth rates. Identify period, geography, category scope, gross/net treatment and methodology. Decide whether they conflict or measure different things.

## Drill 2 — Attractive but false visual

An image looks excellent but shows the wrong product colour and an invented award. Classify each failure and decide whether to regenerate, correct the brief or reject the model.

## Drill 3 — Dashboard mismatch

The dashboard total is 3% higher than the source system. Check duplicates, filters, refunds, date boundaries, currency and joins before publishing.

## Drill 4 — Agent external write

An API request times out after submission. Do not retry. Query the exact object or request ID and classify the outcome as success, failed or uncertain.

## Drill 5 — Day-two evidence status

A deck says a concept is covered, but no video has been processed. Write an answer that explains the concept while keeping it labelled `reference_only`.

## Drill 6 — Author imitation

A draft copies the author's phrases but changes the viewpoint. Another uses new language but preserves the author's judgement. Explain why the second is the stronger continuation.
