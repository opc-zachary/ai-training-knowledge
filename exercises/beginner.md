# Beginner Exercises

## K00 — Ecosystem map

Identify one business owner, one domain expert, one AI operator, one reviewer and one safe test environment for a real problem.

## K01 — Stack classification

Take five AI products you use and label interface, underlying model, tool capability, Agent behaviour and verification method.

## K02 — Workspace brief

Create a folder-bounded task brief for a report. State what the Agent may read, write and never touch.

## K03 — Research question

Rewrite “analyse this brand” into five decision-oriented questions with source and output requirements.

## K04 — Platform evidence

Review 20 authorised posts. Separate post claim, comment evidence, repeated pain point and your interpretation.

## K05 — Voice extraction

Use three authorised texts to identify two high-confidence author rules and one provisional rule.

## K06 — Reference roles

Choose three authorised images and assign layout, subject and scene roles. List what must not be copied.

## K07 — Five-image plan

Plan H01–H05 with five different jobs and at least four main visuals.

## K08 — Five-shot story

Write five three-second shots with start state, action, camera and rejection condition.

## K09 — Data quality profile

For one small table, report rows, keys, missing values, duplicates, invalid types and metric definition.

## K10 — Proof statement

Take one deliverable and write the exact evidence that proves input, output and destination are correct.
