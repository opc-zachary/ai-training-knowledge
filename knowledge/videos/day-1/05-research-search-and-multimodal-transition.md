## Learning outcomes

- Convert a professional method into a tested reusable prompt or Skill.
- Review research sources and inconsistent metric definitions.
- Choose different search/acquisition methods for open web, protected platforms and large datasets.
- Understand why platform content may reveal product pain points missed by formal reports.
- Treat transcription as a specialist model task followed by correction and summarisation.
- Recognise multimodal capability as a foundation for a one-person content operation.

## Four research tiers

| Tier | Use when | Example output | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct model answer | Low-stakes orientation | Definitions, starter questions | Stale or unsupported claim |
| Deep web research | Public sources can answer | Sourced industry/brand report | Conflicting definitions |
| Browser or extension collection | Visible platform content matters | Post/comment dataset | Platform terms and incomplete sample |
| Authorised data provider/API | Scale or history is required | Large structured dataset | Cost, provenance and licence |

## Prompt development loop

```text
Define task
→ ask AI to expose assumptions
→ correct and add professional judgement
→ consolidate into reusable instruction
→ test on a realistic case
→ inspect failure
→ revise rule
→ test again
```

## Data credibility questions

1. Who published the number?
2. What period and geography does it cover?
3. What exactly is counted?
4. Is the metric gross, net, sampled or estimated?
5. Do two sources disagree because one is wrong or because definitions differ?
6. Can a sample row or original page be inspected?
