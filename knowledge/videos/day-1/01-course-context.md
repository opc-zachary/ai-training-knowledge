## What this segment does

This opening explains why the training is being held in a university innovation setting. It introduces the Guangzhou campus, its interdisciplinary technology orientation, research resources, industry collaboration and entrepreneurship programmes. It is context rather than an AI operating tutorial.

## Key ideas

1. AI learning is placed inside a broader innovation ecosystem, not treated as a standalone software class.
2. Interdisciplinary work is a practical operating model: technology, business application, research and entrepreneurship interact.
3. Universities can supply laboratories, talent, partners, funding and a trusted environment for enterprise experimentation.
4. Training can function as an entry point into a longer network of founders, enterprises, researchers and public programmes.

## How to use this context

When adapting the course inside a company, reproduce the ecosystem functions even if a university is absent:

- identify a business sponsor;
- connect domain experts and technical operators;
- provide a safe experimentation environment;
- define a route from prototype to operational adoption;
- create a community that shares results and failures.
