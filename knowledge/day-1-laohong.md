## Reusable methods

### Structured research

1. Define the business question and intended decision.
2. Define the analyst role and scope.
3. Specify acceptable sources, dates and metric definitions.
4. Require a stable output structure.
5. Ask for evidence and contradictions, not only conclusions.
6. Check the most important claims against their original sources.

### Author voice

Analyse multiple representative texts and separate stable judgement patterns from variable format. The reusable layer is the author's viewpoint, attention, value order, evidence habits, reader relationship and expression boundaries. A successful new text should feel like the author without copying an old article's structure.

### Visual production

Use references as visual language, not as material to copy. Decide what each frame must prove before adding copy. For a set, vary subject, camera distance and proof form while keeping shared colour, typography, light and commercial tone.

### Data analysis

Inspect structure and quality before analysis. Confirm metric definitions, then analyse dimensions such as time, region, channel, category and salesperson. Ask change-oriented questions, validate aggregates against sample rows and only then build the dashboard.
