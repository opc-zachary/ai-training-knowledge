# K09 — Business Data Analysis

## Objective

Turn operational rows into reconciled findings and decisions rather than decorative charts.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the original file.
2. Build a data dictionary.
3. Profile rows, dates, keys, missing values and categories.
4. Record cleaning decisions.
5. Define every KPI.
6. Analyse totals and distributions.
7. Analyse changes, exceptions and combinations.
8. Visualise only decision-relevant findings.
9. Reconcile outputs with source samples and totals.
10. State decisions, risks and follow-up data needs.

## Required artefacts

- source manifest;
- data dictionary;
- quality report;
- cleaning log;
- metric definitions;
- analysis workbook or script;
- dashboard;
- reconciliation report;
- decision summary.

## Acceptance

- The same input reproduces the result.
- KPIs have formulas and scope.
- Exclusions and missing data are disclosed.
- Dashboard totals reconcile.
- Findings explain what changed and where.
- Recommendations are proportional to evidence.

## Failure signals

- analysis begins before field meaning is known;
- duplicated rows inflate totals;
- revenue and margin are confused;
- charts have no business question;
- AI confidence replaces reconciliation.
