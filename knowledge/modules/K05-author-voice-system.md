# K05 — Author Voice and PR Writing System

## Objective

Capture how an author notices, reasons and speaks so new writing feels authentic without becoming a template copy.

## Inputs

- multiple representative texts;
- context for platform, audience and business purpose;
- examples the author accepts and rejects;
- current topic and desired reader change.

## Workflow

1. Read all samples before describing the author.
2. Identify cross-topic patterns.
3. Remove false patterns caused by one platform or campaign.
4. Describe observation position, attention, value order and judgement.
5. Describe reader relationship and emotional boundary.
6. Separate must-keep rules from variable structure.
7. Draft a new topic using the system.
8. Evaluate authenticity and novelty separately.

## Deliverables

- one-sentence author definition;
- stable rules with evidence and confidence;
- variable-form guidance;
- imitation traps;
- reusable writing brief;
- self-review rubric.

## Acceptance

If familiar phrases are removed, the angle and judgement still feel like the author. If the structure changes, the identity remains recognisable.

## Failure signals

- copying catchphrases;
- forcing every article into one structure;
- using vague traits without decision rules;
- treating one emotional article as the permanent personality;
- producing polished but positionless PR language.
