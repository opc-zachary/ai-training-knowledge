# K08 — AI Video Production

## Objective

Produce a coherent sequence by controlling character, scene, shot and usable yield.

## Workflow

1. Define message, audience, duration and channel.
2. Build character and scene bibles.
3. Write a shot list with start and end states.
4. Create reference frames or storyboard.
5. Select model by duration, adherence, cost and reference support.
6. Generate short manageable clips.
7. Reject identity, physics, product or continuity failures.
8. Edit selected clips into narrative order.
9. Add sound and captions with separate review.
10. Export and inspect the final channel format.

## Deliverables

- brief;
- character/scene bible;
- shot list;
- storyboard;
- generation log;
- selected clips;
- edit decision list;
- final video and QA report.

## Acceptance

- Character and product remain recognisable.
- Motion and camera support the story.
- Each shot has a narrative function.
- Artefacts do not distract at normal playback.
- Captions and audio are accurate.
- Effective cost is measured by usable output.

## Failure signals

- one long generation with no rejection plan;
- impressive shots that do not connect;
- no identity or scene bible;
- cost measured without failure rate;
- generation treated as the end of editing.
