# K03 — Evidence-led Brand and Industry Research

## Objective

Produce a decision-oriented report whose important claims can be traced and challenged.

## Inputs

- target brand, company, product or market;
- stakeholder and decision;
- geography and time range;
- comparable competitors;
- allowed source classes;
- output format and deadline.

## Workflow

1. Convert the business decision into research questions.
2. Define the analyst role and perspective.
3. Build an evidence plan before searching.
4. Collect source title, publisher, date and URL with each claim.
5. Separate fact, interpretation and recommendation.
6. Resolve metric-definition conflicts.
7. Structure evolution, portfolio, communication, competition and risk.
8. Run contradiction and missing-evidence checks.
9. Produce executive and detailed views.

## Deliverables

- research brief;
- source ledger;
- claim-evidence table;
- full report;
- executive summary;
- uncertainty and follow-up list.

## Acceptance

An informed reviewer can trace the major claims, understand conflicting numbers and see how the evidence supports the decision.

## Failure signals

- long report with no decision;
- competitor list without comparison logic;
- statistics without definition or date;
- recommendation unrelated to evidence;
- polished HTML used to hide weak sourcing.
