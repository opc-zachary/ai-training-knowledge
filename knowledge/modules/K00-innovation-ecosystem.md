# K00 — Innovation and Learning Ecosystem

## Objective

Create the organisational conditions that allow AI experiments to become repeatable business capability.

## Inputs

- business sponsors and priority problems;
- domain experts and operators;
- data, tools and test environment;
- research, training and external partners;
- decision and review authority.

## Workflow

1. List the business problems worth solving.
2. Name one owner for each problem.
3. Match domain knowledge with AI operating capability.
4. Create a bounded place for experiments.
5. Define how a prototype becomes an approved workflow.
6. Hold regular demonstrations with evidence and failure notes.
7. Capture reusable methods into Skills, templates or SOPs.

## Deliverables

- AI opportunity map;
- owner and reviewer matrix;
- experiment backlog;
- monthly demonstration record;
- promotion criteria from trial to operation.

## Acceptance

The ecosystem is working when a useful experiment has an owner, evidence, reviewer, adoption path and maintained reusable method—not merely an enthusiastic demo.

## Common failures

- training without a business problem;
- tools without owners;
- prototypes without production path;
- no distinction between experiment and approved process;
- one expert becoming the only person who can operate the system.
