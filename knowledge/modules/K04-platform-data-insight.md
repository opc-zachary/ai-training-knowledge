# K04 — Platform Data and Consumer Insight

## Objective

Turn permitted platform observations into product, audience and content insight without confusing a convenience sample with the whole market.

## Workflow

1. Define the question: product pain point, audience language, competitor content or topic demand.
2. Define query, account/sample scope and period.
3. Record which content was visible and collected.
4. Deduplicate posts and normalise engagement fields.
5. Classify themes, situations, objections and desired outcomes.
6. Read comments separately from post copy.
7. Compare high-engagement and ordinary posts.
8. Return to representative originals to verify interpretation.
9. State sampling and access limitations.

## Insight structure

| Evidence | Interpretation | Business implication | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeated complaint | Unmet use expectation | Product or service improvement |  |
| Repeated phrase | Audience language | Copy and search term |  |
| Repeated scenario | Use context | Creative or channel plan |  |
| Engagement anomaly | Potential resonance | Test, not conclusion |  |

## Acceptance

- Data provenance and period are recorded.
- Duplicates and missing fields are visible.
- Insights cite representative examples.
- The report distinguishes frequency from business importance.
- Sampling limitations are explicit.

## Failure signals

- treating likes as sales;
- analysing only viral posts;
- ignoring comments and negative evidence;
- presenting a small collection as market share;
- collecting data before defining the decision.
